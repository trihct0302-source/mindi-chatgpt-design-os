# Skill — Visual Audit

## Trigger
Use to assess screenshots or rendered designs for visual quality.

## Checks
Hierarchy, alignment, spacing, typography, color, contrast, imagery, cropping, density, consistency, and visual emphasis.

## Output format
Finding ID | location | observed evidence | expected result | severity | impact | recommendation | retest criteria.

## Constraint
Do not infer pixel-level measurements from images when the source or measurement is unavailable.
