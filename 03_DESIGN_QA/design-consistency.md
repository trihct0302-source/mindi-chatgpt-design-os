# Skill — Design Consistency

## Trigger
Use to compare multiple pages or components for adherence to shared patterns.

## Procedure
Compare typography, color roles, spacing, buttons, form controls, navigation, cards, iconography, states, and content conventions against approved references.

## Output
Consistency matrix with canonical reference, deviations, severity, impact, and recommended alignment.

## Constraint
Do not treat a visual difference as a defect when it is an intentional, documented pattern.
