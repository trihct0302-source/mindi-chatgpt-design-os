# Workflow — Figma Design Assistant

## Purpose
Turn an approved design brief and accessible Figma source into a structured understanding of the design, UX/UI review findings, and actionable proposals.

## Inputs
- Figma file URL and relevant page/frame/node IDs.
- Approved brief, audience, goals, constraints, and priority journeys.
- Brand/design references and review criteria.

## Process
1. Confirm access and identify the exact source and frames.
2. Inventory pages, frames, components, variables, and relevant prototypes.
3. Map the design against the brief and intended user journey.
4. Review information hierarchy, navigation, CTA clarity, content, responsive intent, and states.
5. Separate observed facts from inferences and unknowns.
6. Rank issues by severity and user/business impact.
7. Produce recommendations and a review-ready change proposal.

## Outputs
- Source inventory.
- UX/UI review report with evidence and severity.
- Design opportunities and prioritized proposals.
- Questions/decisions required from the project owner.

## Guardrails
- No Figma edits under L0–L2.
- Do not infer frame content if the source is inaccessible.
- Do not change product requirements or approved copy without approval.
- L3 changes require explicit target and scope approval.
