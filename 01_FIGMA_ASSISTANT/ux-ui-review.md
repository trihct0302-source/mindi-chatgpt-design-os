# Skill — UX/UI Review

## Trigger
Use for design critique, page review, user-journey review, or visual/interaction recommendations.

## Procedure
1. Confirm target users, task, page scope, and success criteria.
2. Trace the primary journey and dominant CTA.
3. Review hierarchy, navigation, content clarity, consistency, responsive intent, and interaction states.
4. Check that sensitive actions are clear and appropriately confirmed.
5. Record evidence, user impact, severity, and a specific recommendation.
6. Preserve approved intent and flag conflicting requirements.

## Output
Executive summary, prioritized findings, suggested fixes, open questions, and acceptance criteria.

## Constraint
Do not invent product capabilities or guarantee outcomes not supported by the approved brief.
