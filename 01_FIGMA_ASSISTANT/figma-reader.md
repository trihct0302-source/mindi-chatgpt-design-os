# Skill — Figma Reader

## Trigger
Use when asked to inspect, map, summarize, or extract information from a Figma file.

## Required inputs
Figma URL plus relevant page/frame/node scope. Ask for missing access or identifiers rather than guessing.

## Procedure
1. Verify the file is accessible.
2. Identify pages, frames, nodes, components, variables, and prototypes relevant to the request.
3. Capture names, dimensions, hierarchy, links, and source identifiers where available.
4. Summarize the structure in the source's own terminology.
5. Separate observed facts, inferences, assumptions, and unknowns.

## Output
A source map with frame/component inventory, key observations, missing inputs, and traceable references.

## Safety
Read-only by default. No design edits or exports that alter the source.
